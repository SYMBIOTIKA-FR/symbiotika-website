ABOUT SYMBIOTIKA

An ongoing and living practice
of intercultural togetherness.

Where It Began...

Symbiotika began from the need to create space for encounters between people, artistic practices, cultures and places. Rather than defining one fixed way of working, it creates conditions where different perspectives can meet and affect one another.

The Anatomy of Our Name

Symbiotika comes from the idea of symbiosis: different entities living, changing and evolving through relation. For us, connection is not simply about existing side by side. An encounter leaves a trace and creates something that did not exist before.

What Brought Us Together

Symbiotika is an ongoing and living practice. It grows through conversations, collaborations, shared experiences and unexpected encounters. Its identity is not fixed; it continues to become through the people and practices that enter it.

Our Extended Family...

Symbiotika develops through an expanding network of artists, collaborators, organisations, spaces and communities. Each relationship contributes to the platform and leaves its own trace within it.

The People We Grew With

Our partners help create the conditions for exchange, production and experimentation. They are not only supporters of individual projects, but part of the wider ecology through which Symbiotika grows.

Why We Exist

Symbiotika creates spaces for artistic exchange across cultures, practices and disciplines. We support encounters that can lead to new collaborations, shared knowledge and forms of practice that could not emerge alone.
