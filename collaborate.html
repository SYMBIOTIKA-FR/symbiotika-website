BE PART OF IT

Whether you are an artist, an art lover, or simply curious and looking to discover new worlds, we invite you to become part of our community. No prerequisites are needed except curiosity. If you have that, let’s go together.

Artists

Write us a few words about yourself, your artistic practice, where you are based, and how you imagine collaborating with or being supported by our network.

Individuals

Leave us your contact information if you would like to stay in the loop about upcoming events, gatherings, workshops and open calls.

Volunteers

Tell us about your interests, skills, passions and how you would like to contribute to our projects and activities.

Programmers & Curators

Write to us about upcoming programmes, the artistic practices you are looking for, and possible ways we can collaborate.

Venues & Spaces

Send us a few lines about your space and how you imagine hosting or developing projects together.

Sponsors & Donors

Let us know how you would like to support our activities, artists and community, and we will be happy to imagine meaningful ways of collaborating with you.

None of the Above?

Write to us anyway. Let’s see where we can go together.
